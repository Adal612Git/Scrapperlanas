## 💡 Pro Tips for Winning
1. **Speed is everything:** Respond within 15 minutes of the alert.
2. **Show, Don't Tell:** If you have a GitHub repo or a recorded Loom of a similar bot, send the link.
3. **The "Call to Action":** Always end with a question:
   - "When can we start?"
   - "Do you have the API documentation?"
   - "Can we jump on a 5-min call?"
4. **Author Trust:** Check the `Author` field in the report. If they have high karma, they are likely more serious buyers.
